About Kishan Rahul

Drone Filmmaker · Stock Footage Creator · Chennai, India

The Story

It started in late 2018 with a DJI Mavic Air and a curiosity about what India looks like from above. The first clips uploaded to Shutterstock started generating revenue almost immediately — proof that professional aerial footage of Indian cities was in high demand and undersupplied.

Over the years the library grew to over 5,000+ clips, with 90% being Indian footage shot across cities and locations throughout the country — distributed through BlackBox.global with the majority live online. Today the equipment has evolved to the DJI Mavic Air 3 and Mini 4 Pro, and the footage has appeared in productions ranging from Amazon Prime originals to IPL advertisements to major corporate campaigns.

Why Direct?

This store offers professional 4K footage directly to creators and businesses at India-friendly pricing — faster, simpler and more affordable than international platforms. No subscriptions, no USD pricing, no waiting. Browse, preview, buy and download instantly.
